What is UNIFOAM?

UNIFOAM is the meeting point where the technical rigor of polyurethane insulation meets the strength and dignity of Hispanic skilled labor. We are not an office: we are the applicators who climb roofs in the Texas summer heat, and the ones who turned a hard trade into solid, respected businesses.

Field calibration

Spray foam tips, in your language.

The rig doesn't forgive mistakes. Three variables that separate an average job from a perfect application.

Parameter 01

Temperature

Master the heat of the foam

Constant monitoring of heated hoses, thermal control of the tanks, and reading the substrate. Adjusting for humidity and season keeps the foam from crystallizing or pulling away.

Hoses · tanks · substrate per system
Parameter 02

Mix ratio

Exact ratio, exact result

Keeping the 1:1 relationship of Side A (isocyanate) and Side B (resin), with calibrated equipment, guarantees a perfect cure with no sticky spots.

Chemical ratio 1:1 · A / B
Parameter 03

Pressure

Steady pressure, even application

Firm trigger, constant distance, rhythmic pass speed: that's how you lay a flawless fan pattern without excess overspray mist.

Journey & brotherhood

From tech to contractor.

This story wasn't written in air-conditioned offices. It started with a sweat-soaked coverall, a mask strapped tight, and a bent back inside the blazing attics of Central Texas.

A tech who knew the value of every gallon, who learned to purge a clogged gun, and who earned builders' respect with square footage applied to perfection. Through hard work and nights of study, the employee's gun became his own trailer and a contractor's license.

René Navarrete — that tech who became a contractor and a mentor — helped start UNIFOAM. Today he shares what he knows, because he understood that when the Hispanic contractor raises his standard, the whole industry gains dignity.

"He comes to make the Latino contractor grow."

— from the UNIFOAM anthem

01

The helper

Hauling hoses, masking windows, scraping overspray, and learning how to breathe in the attic.

02

The applicator tech

Gun in hand. Mastering density, thickness per pass, and fine-tuning the machine.

03

The rig leader

Rig maintenance, set inventory, and quality control on the job site.

04

The owner-contractor

Professional estimating, independent rig, crews to lead, and mentorship for the next generation.
